# Deep POV & Prose Generation Mastery Handbook

This reference guides AI agents when drafting long-form novel chapters to ensure prose reads like masterwork literature rather than generic AI output.

---

## 1. Eradication of Sensory Filter Words

Filter words place an artificial glass barrier between the reader and the protagonist's experience. Eradicate them with extreme prejudice:

| Weak / Filtered Prose (Banned) | Deep POV / Immediate Somatic Reality (Mandatory) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *She saw the door creak open.* | *The oak door groaned on rusty iron hinges.* |
| *He felt a sharp pain in his rib.* | *A splintered rib stabbed into his right lung with every ragged breath.* |
| *She heard footsteps echoing in the hall.* | *Boots scraped across stone tiles, rhythmic and unhurried.* |
| *He wondered if she was lying to him.* | *Her fingers picked obsessively at her hemline. The same tell she had before abandoning him in Vienna.* |
| *She noticed how cold the basement was.* | *Frost crunched beneath her boots; her breath bloomed in ragged gray plumes.* |

---

## 2. Somatic Neurobiology (The 3-Beat Reaction Sequence)

Human beings do not process traumatic or shocking stimuli by delivering articulate verbal dialogue first. The neurological sequence must be respected:

1. **Beat 1: Involuntary Somatic / Visceral Reflex**:
   - Pupils dilating, adrenaline spike, stomach plummeting, diaphragm freezing, skin prickling.
2. **Beat 2: Instinctive Physical Reaction**:
   - Snatching the hand back, bracing against the wall, dropping the glass, freezing mid-breath.
3. **Beat 3: Conscious Thought & Spoken Dialogue**:
   - The rational brain catches up; calculating the lie, uttering the defensive retort.

---

## 3. Subtextual Dialogue & Conversational Power Dynamics

Dialogue in fiction is not for transmitting factual information to the reader; dialogue is **tactical maneuvering between two competing agendas**:
- **Rule of Asymmetry**: Characters rarely answer the question that was asked. They deflect, counter-interrogate, change the subject, or use sarcasm.
- **Prop Business (Stage Movement)**: Ground every dialogue exchange in tactile physical actions (polishing a glass, checking a blade's edge, slicing an onion, untangling a necklace).
- **The Unsaid**: What the characters are deliberately avoiding saying is twice as important as what they say aloud.

---

## 4. Calibrated Cliffhanger Cadence by Target Platform

Every chapter must end with narrative kinetic momentum:
- **Traditional Print**: Thematic resonance, an unresolved moral dilemma, or an unexpected arrival that recontextualizes the current plan.
- **Royal Road / Progression Serial**: A tangible breakthrough, a loot drop, a sudden raid warning, or an encounter with a superior realm cultivator.
- **Romance / Dark Romance**: An agonizing near-kiss, an accidental touch, an overheard betrayal, or the appearance of an ex-rival.
- **Paywall Serial (KBM / Fizzo)**: An explosive confrontation, a slap, an ultimatum, or an eviction notice that forces readers to spend coins for the next chapter.
