# StoryCraft Skills for Gemini & Antigravity

StoryCraft equips your agent with 20 professional fiction writing and novel architecture skills.

## Core Rules

1. Before generating chapters or plotting scenes, check for existing project state in `story-state.md` and canon in `story-bible.md`.
2. Activate `using-storycraft` or `novel-brainstorming` when initiating new projects or diagnosing creative roadblocks.
3. Eliminate sensory filter words ("saw", "felt", "heard", "wondered") and maintain Deep Point of View (Deep POV) across all narrative drafting.
4. Enforce physical wound, inventory, and timeline continuity via `novel-continuity-checker`.
5. Update `story-state.md` and generate the Next Session Bootstrap Prompt upon concluding any writing task.
